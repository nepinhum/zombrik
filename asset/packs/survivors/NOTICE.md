# asset/packs/survivors — free to use and redistribute

Overhead survivors (unarmed, pistol, rifle, shotgun, SMG), a zombie, and gun
sprites in top and side view.

**Free to use and redistribute**, including in this repository.

`tools/face_east.py` turns these to face +x and writes them to
`mod/assets/characters/`, which the game serves as `zombrik/characters/*.png`.
